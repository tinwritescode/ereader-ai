Import, read, choose your time

Four screens, one per step. Book data is real: chapter lengths come from the uploaded EPUB at 200 words per minute. The yellow bar means one thing everywhere: the part of the chapter you will actually read.

How to suggest shortening a chapter

The mockup offers it in two places, both quiet:

  • The bar on every chapter row makes long chapters obvious, which is the cue for itself.
  • Tapping any chapter opens the length sheet. The app remembers the last choice (for example "about half") and preselects it next time, so a reader who always shortens can do it in one tap.

The options are relative to the chapter: full, about 50%, about 25%, with a 2-minute floor. Chapters under 5 minutes skip the sheet and open the original directly.
